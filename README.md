## About Me
I am a Civil Engineer and MSc in Environmental and Sanitary Engineering, currently pursuing a PhD in Environmental Sciences at the Federal University of Goiás (UFG), Brazil.

My research focuses on satellite-based precipitation estimation, remote sensing, hydrology, and climate applications in the Cerrado biome.

## Research Interests

- Satellite precipitation products and validation
- GPM IMERG and precipitation estimation
- Remote sensing and geospatial analysis
- Hydrology and urban drainage
- Climate variability and change
