### About Me
Civil Engineer and MSc in Environmental and Sanitary Engineering, currently pursuing a PhD in Environmental Sciences at the Federal University of Goiás (UFG), Brazil.

My research focuses on satellite-based precipitation estimation, remote sensing, hydrology, and climate applications across the Cerrado biome.

### Research Interests
- Satellite precipitation products and spatial validation
- GPM IMERG algorithms and performance evaluation
- Remote sensing and geospatial data science
- Hydrology and urban drainage systems
- Climate variability and environmental impacts
